About me

Infrastructure that holds up,
even when users attack it.

I'm Radharaman Sethi, an infrastructure engineer with nearly three years of hands-on work on OpenStack private clouds. Most of my days are spent inside Nova, Neutron, Cinder, Glance and Keystone, plus the RabbitMQ, MariaDB and HAProxy that hold them together.

I studied Cyber Security, so I build with an attacker's mindset. My current work supports a multi-tenant cyber range, where tenants are expected to probe the platform. That puts isolation, network design and careful troubleshooting at the centre of everything I do.

Next up: Kubernetes. I'm preparing for the CKA so I can bring container orchestration together with OpenStack depth for platform engineering.
