About me

Curious by default,
builder by habit.

I'm Radharaman Sethi, a software developer who loves the whole journey of an idea — from a scribble in a notebook to a container running in production.

I spend most of my time in the JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystem, building with React and Next.js, and I have a soft spot for Docker, automation and anything that makes a team's day a little smoother.

When I'm not coding, I'm writing about what I've learned — because explaining something is the fastest way to find out whether you actually understand it.
